Cold Starts is a critical concept in cloud-native, distributed, or high-performance systems. This note explains its meaning, why it exists, and how it influences platform architecture and performance.

- This concept links to deeper topics such as scheduling, IO optimizations, metadata layers, or coordination primitives.

## Why it matters
A solid grasp of **Cold Starts** helps engineers make better architectural decisions, tune performance, and build resilient distributed applications.
