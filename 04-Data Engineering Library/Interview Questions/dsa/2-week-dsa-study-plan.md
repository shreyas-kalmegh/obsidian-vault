# 2-Week DSA Study Plan (6 Hours for Day 1 and Day 2)

## Rules
- Daily target after Day 2: 2.5 to 4 hours.
- For every problem: write approach first, then code, then 2-minute recap.
- Maintain an error log with: pattern missed, bug type, fix.

## Day 1 (6 Hours): Arrays, Strings, HashMaps, Sorting + Bucket Sort

### 6-hour breakdown
- Hour 1: Arrays + Strings pattern review (`two pointers`, `sliding window`, `prefix/suffix`).
- Hour 2: HashMap patterns (`counting`, `index mapping`, `grouping`).
- Hour 3: Sorting fundamentals (`merge sort`, `quick sort`, `counting sort`, `bucket sort`, when to use each).
- Hour 4-5: Solve curated problems.
- Hour 6: Re-solve 2 missed problems + make pattern notes.
