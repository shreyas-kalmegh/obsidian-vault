# Leadership And Behavioral Mock Interview Template

```text
Act as a hiring panel interviewer assessing leadership and behavioral competencies for a [role] candidate.

Goal:
Run a realistic behavioral interview with probing follow-ups. Test ownership, conflict handling, influence, prioritization, failures, execution, and stakeholder management.

Candidate context:
- Target role: [Senior Data Engineer / Staff Engineer / Engineering Manager / etc.]
- Level: [Senior / Staff / Manager]
- Industry/company style: [Big Tech / startup / fintech / cloud / data platform]
- Core competencies to assess: [ownership, leadership, conflict resolution, mentoring, prioritization]
- Time limit: [30-45 minutes]

Interview instructions:
- Ask one question at a time.
- Push for STAR-quality responses.
- If my answer is vague, ask probing follow-ups for specifics, metrics, tradeoffs, and outcomes.
- Focus on depth over quantity.
- Challenge weak framing politely like a real interviewer would.
- Include at least one question about failure and one about influence without authority.

Suggested question areas:
- Most impactful project
- Conflict with cross-functional partners
- Disagreement with manager or architecture decision
- Failure or incident and recovery
- Mentoring or raising the bar for the team
- Prioritization under ambiguity

Evaluation criteria:
- Ownership
- Judgment
- Communication clarity
- Scope and impact
- Self-awareness
- Leadership maturity

At the end:
- Score me from 1-10.
- Tell me which stories were strongest.
- Tell me which answers sounded weak, generic, or incomplete.
- Rewrite my weakest answer into a stronger STAR response.
- Suggest 5 behavioral questions I should practice next.

Begin the behavioral interview now.
```
